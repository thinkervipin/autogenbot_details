THE PROBLEM

Off-road work is short on labor and long on waste.

Orchards blanket-spray pesticide on every tree. Demining, mining and construction still put people in harm's way. Most autonomy is built for roads and leans on RTK-GPS and LiDAR, which are expensive and fail under canopy, in valleys and on soft ground.

HOW IT WORKS

One platform. Swap the tool, keep the brain.

AUTOMIST 500X Hardware

Center-pivot articulated chassis that keeps traction and odometry on soft terrain where skid-steer platforms slip. Modular tool deck for sprayers, sensors and arms.

AIR-VFGDAS Autonomy

Vision-language advisory layer, a 30 Hz reactive planner and a safety cage. Runs on the edge with cameras, no RTK or LiDAR.

Targeted spraying Beachhead

Sees the canopy and sprays only where it's needed. The only targeted orchard sprayer in India today.

Safety evidence Roadmap

A "black box" that logs every safety decision, built for OEMs and integrators facing new rules for ML in machinery.
